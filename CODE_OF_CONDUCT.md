# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on the project.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- discuss technical disagreements without personal attacks;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or deliberately disruptive behavior;
- respect privacy and do not post credentials, signing material, private logs, or personal data;
- keep issue and pull-request discussions relevant and actionable;
- assume good intent while still giving clear technical feedback.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior repeatedly undermines a safe and productive project environment.

Security-sensitive reports should follow `SECURITY.md` instead of being posted publicly.
